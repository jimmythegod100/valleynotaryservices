**[OPEN — face to camera, warm but professional. Modest background: desk with stamp/seal visible.]**

If you're in Modesto or anywhere in Stanislaus County and you need documents notarized — maybe you're closing on a home, signing estate papers, or handling a power of attorney — you probably don't want to waste an afternoon driving to a storefront or waiting in line.

I'm [YOUR NAME], a California notary public with Valley Notary Services. We are a mobile notary. That means we come to you.

---

**[B-ROLL: calendar, stacked envelopes, keys on table — suggest deadline pressure.]**

Most people call us when they're already on a timeline. A closing date from escrow. A refinance that has to fund. A family member in the hospital who needs documents signed today. Or an attorney who sent over a trust packet and said, "Get this notarized this week."

You need someone who shows up on time, handles the documents carefully, and communicates clearly from the first email to the last signature. That's the standard we work to every day.

---

**[BACK TO CAMERA — then cut to mobile kit: stamp, journal, pens.]**

Here's what we do.

**Loan signing and real estate closings** — escrow packages, purchase loans, refinances, and HELOC signings. We understand that lender timelines matter. Missing a signature or initial can delay your closing, so we pay attention to the details in the package.

**Estate planning documents** — wills, trusts, healthcare directives, and related acknowledgments. Often that's at your kitchen table or your attorney's office, with family members who need a calm, professional presence. We perform notarial acts only. We don't give legal advice — we're not attorneys — but we make the signing process smooth and by the book.

**General notary work** — affidavits, powers of attorney, bills of sale, deeds, and other everyday California notarial acts.

And because we're mobile, we meet you where it's convenient: your home, your office, a hospital, a coffee shop — anywhere across Modesto, Ceres, Turlock, Salida, Riverbank, Oakdale, and the broader Central Valley.

---

**[B-ROLL: hands guiding to signature line on BLANK form; stamp impression; commission certificate on wall.]**

A few things that matter when you're choosing a notary.

I'm commissioned as a California notary public, authorized for mobile service across the state. For loan signings, we're familiar with the flow escrow and title companies expect — sign here, initial there, return the completed package on schedule.

We treat your documents with care. That means valid ID for every signer, documents that are unsigned when we arrive unless your instructions say otherwise, and following any special instructions from your escrow officer or attorney.

If something in the package looks wrong, we'll pause and tell you to contact the company that sent it. We'd rather slow down for five minutes than create a problem on closing day.

**What to have ready before we arrive:**

1. Valid, government-issued photo ID for every person who will sign.

3. Any notes from escrow, your lender, or your attorney about how the signing should be handled.

That's it. We'll bring the notarial supplies.
