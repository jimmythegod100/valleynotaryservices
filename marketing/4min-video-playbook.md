### Ideal client (who converts)

1. **Homeowners / buyers** — escrow closing, refinance, HELOC; need someone reliable who respects lender timelines.
2. **Families** — estate planning (will, trust, healthcare directive) at home; want calm, clear guidance (not legal advice).
3. **Busy professionals** — POA, affidavits, bills of sale; need evening or on-site appointments in Modesto area.
4. **Referral partners** — escrow officers, loan officers, estate attorneys who need a dependable mobile notary.

**Not on site (gaps to fill before filming):** phone number, notary public name/face, RON status, pricing. Script uses email CTA only until phone is added.

## Why ~4 minutes

- Long enough to explain services, process, and area — builds trust for a high-stakes purchase (signing).
- Short enough for YouTube, Facebook, and Google Business Profile without drop-off.
- Target spoken pace: **130–150 words/min → ~520–600 words total**.

```
Most people call when they're already on a deadline — a closing date,
```

```
We specialize in loan signing and real estate closings — escrow packages,

And for everyday needs — affidavits, powers of attorney, bills of sale, 
and deeds — we meet you at a location that works: home, office, hospital,
```

```
I'm commissioned as a California notary public. I don't give legal advice — 
I perform notarial acts in accordance with California law — but I do take 
document handling seriously, especially on loan packages where details matter.
```

```
Before we meet, have valid photo ID for every signer, your unsigned documents, 
and any special instructions from escrow or your attorney.

Email us the document type, preferred time, and location — we'll confirm 
availability promptly.
```

```
Visit valleynotaryservices.com or email info@valleynotaryservices.com.

Use the subject line "Notary Appointment Request" — that helps us prioritize 
your signing.

Valley Notary Services — mobile notary public for Modesto and the Central Valley.
```

## Production stack (one primary + one fallback)

### Primary: **iPhone + CapCut Desktop**

**Why (not Remotion for video #1):**

- Local notary buyers hire **people**, not motion graphics. Face + real stamp/seal beats avatar explainers.
- CapCut Desktop: fast auto-captions (critical for mobile/social mute viewing), 16:9 export, no subscription required for basics.
- Turnaround: shoot one afternoon, edit same day.

**Settings**

| Setting | Value |
|---------|--------|
| Capture | iPhone 1080p60 or 4K30, rear camera for B-roll; front or tripod for talking head |
| Audio | Quiet room; clip-on lav if available; avoid wind outdoors |
| Edit | CapCut Desktop — 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 |
| Captions | Auto-generate → fix legal terms (notary, HELOC, Stanislaus) |
| Lower thirds | Navy `#0f172a` bar, gold `#d97706` accent — match site |
| Music | Optional: low-volume royalty-free; duck under voice |

**Workflow**

1. Read script aloud once; trim to ~4:00.
2. Film A-roll (talking head) in 4–6 chunks matching beats above.
3. Film B-roll list below (10–15 clips, 3–5 sec each).
4. Import to CapCut → align cuts every 3–6 sec on beat changes.
5. Export → **watch full video with sound** before publishing (Review Gate).

### Fallback: **Remotion + Edge TTS** (`explainer-do`)

Use when no one can appear on camera yet:

```bash
~/.organized/bin/explainer-do setup   # first time only
~/.organized/bin/explainer-do magic "4 minute explainer for Valley Notary Services mobile notary in Modesto CA" ~/Desktop/valley-notary-explainer.mp4
```

Or build a brief JSON with brand colors `#0f172a` / `#d97706`, site screenshots, and the script from `video-01-script.md`. Good for a placeholder until real footage exists — **less converting** than face-on-camera for this niche.

**Do not default to DaVinci** for video #1 — overkill unless you need heavy color grading or multicam.

## Shot list & B-roll (trustworthy, not stock-generic)

Film these yourself — even phone footage reads authentic.

| Clip | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Notary stamp on document (no PII visible) | Proof of role |
| Embosser or seal close-up | Trust signal |
| Commission certificate (name visible, number blurred if preferred) | Credential |
| Mobile kit: blue pen, journal, thumbprint pad if used | Professional setup |
| Driving to appointment / parking at residential street (generic) | “We come to you” |
| Kitchen or dining table signing setup | Home visits |
| Office conference table with doc stack | Business clients |
| Hands pointing to signature line (blank form) | Process |
| Calendar or phone showing “appointment confirmed” (mock) | Responsiveness |
| Screen record: valleynotaryservices.com + mailto link | Digital CTA |
| Car trunk or bag packed with supplies | Mobile service |
| Evening golden-hour exterior (Modesto landmark optional) | Evening availability |

**Avoid:** cheesy stock handshakes, generic courthouse footage from other states, documents with real names/account numbers.

## Distribution & lead capture

### Where to post (priority order)

1. **Google Business Profile** — upload full video; add to “Services” and posts. Drives local “mobile notary Modesto” intent.
2. **YouTube** — title: `Mobile Notary Modesto CA | Valley Notary Services`; description with email + cities; link to site.
3. **Facebook Page + local groups** (where allowed) — Ceres/Modesto community groups; follow group rules.
4. **Instagram Reels / TikTok** — cut 30–60 sec hooks from same footage; CTA in caption.
5. **Email signature + referral partners** — send link to escrow/title contacts.

### CTA (match the site)

**Primary:** Email **info@valleynotaryservices.com** with subject **Notary Appointment Request**

Prefilled body fields (from site):

- Name
- Phone
- Preferred date / time
- Location
- Document type

```
Valley Notary Services
valleynotaryservices.com
info@valleynotaryservices.com
Subject: Notary Appointment Request
Modesto · Stanislaus County · Central Valley
```
